# 04 — Augmented Humans, Not Agentic Replacement

🎬 Video: [`video-prompts.md#04-augmented-humans`](video-prompts.md#04-augmented-humans)

---

The CDP is built for **augmented humans** — not for autonomous agents with a human rubber-stamping at the margins. This is the single most important design commitment in the whole protocol, and it's worth being precise about what it means.

> "The combination of humans and AI can yield more effective results compared to the separate performance of each component alone." — Loske & Klumpp, 2021

The augmented-humans frame holds that AI should be grafted onto human capacity to extend what a person can do — without removing them from the doing. It's a deliberate contrast to the "full-agentic-with-human-in-the-loop" drift, where the system acts and the human is reduced to approving or correcting at the margins.

## Where the CDP sits

- **The author writes the spec.** Not the AI.
- **The AI personas surface what the author missed.** They attack; they don't author.
- **The author responds, decides, and integrates.** Every resolution requires explicit confirmation — silence never resolves a vulnerability (more on this in Lesson 09).

In the vocabulary of human-AI collaboration research, the CDP sits at **Interdependency** (not Competition, Supplementation, or the further step, Hybrid): the persona has nothing to attack without the author's spec, and the author can't see their own blind spots without the persona's structured pressure. Neither side produces a hardened spec alone — and the protocol deliberately stops short of Hybrid, where authorship itself would dissolve into a fused human-AI process.

## Who does what

| Role | Who plays it | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Persona-as-AI | The AI | Creator — generates critiques, surfaces what the author didn't see |
| Facilitator-as-AI | The AI (a different function, same or different runtime) | Coordinator + Perfectionist — orchestrates rounds, enforces the rules, mediates transitions |
| The human author | You | Judge — none of the above. Answers, accepts or rejects, declares the spec hardened |

Mapped onto a simpler split: the AI does **mechanical** work (counting, scanning, logging) and **analytical** work (finding patterns in attacks, drafting structured critiques); the human does **intuitive judgment** — the differentiation claim, the final call, the things no model size unlocks. The CDP does not invert this. A protocol that does invert it has quietly crossed from augmentation into automation-with-supervision — a different system, with different risks.

## What this buys you

Two concrete outcomes:

- **Reduced elaboration time** — what would take weeks of asynchronous peer review compresses into hours of structured demolition.
- **Increased accuracy** — blind spots that would otherwise surface only at execution time get forced into visibility at spec time.

Better specs, faster, without surrendering authorship. That's the trade the CDP is offering — and it's a real trade, not a free lunch: it requires you to stay engaged with every round, not delegate the review and come back for a summary.
